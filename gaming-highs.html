Gaming Highs

football.manybumps.com

Overview

Drop a link to a full game and get back a highlight video of every scoring play. It started as the Madden-only bot; on 2026-09-15 it became a multi-game highlights bot with a website. No weeks, no roundups, no voting - just highlights.

🔗

Link in, highlights out

YouTube, Twitch, a direct video link, or a video file dropped in Discord or uploaded on the site.

🎮

Many games

Madden (calibrated), NFL broadcasts, NBA 2K, EA FC / FIFA, MLB. The game is guessed from the title; one click to change it.

👀

Score-change detection

OCR reads the scoreboard every 2 seconds; a change that's legal for the sport becomes a clip around the play.

🎬

Posted everywhere

Highlights channel (with a Key Plays button), YouTube if connected, and the website gallery.

How it works

  1. Someone posts a game link or video file in the submissions channel (or on the website with the submit key).
  2. The bot reads the title, guesses the game, and asks to confirm. Nothing pressed for 90 seconds = it starts with the guess.
  3. The video is downloaded, the scoreboard is OCR'd, scoring plays are found, clips are cut and stitched, a thumbnail is made, and the download + clip pieces are deleted.
  4. The finished video is posted in the highlights channel, uploaded to YouTube, and listed at football.manybumps.com. About an hour per game; one at a time at low CPU priority so Tv Kitty and Purrl always come first; the rest queue.

Website

The gallery is public. Submitting from the site needs the key an admin creates with !webkey (each game costs an hour of CPU, so it's not open to the internet). Uploads up to 6 GB.

Link screening

Every submitted link (Discord or website) is screened before the bot touches it: only http(s), no private or bare-IP addresses, no shortened links, no throwaway domains, no executable downloads, no fake Discord/Steam/Nitro domains. Add SAFE_BROWSING_API_KEY to .env to also check Google Safe Browsing. Refused links get the reason back. Shared linkcheck.py with the other bots.

Adding or calibrating a game

Each game is a profile in src/profiles.py: where the score sits on screen (percent of the frame), which team codes to expect, which score jumps are legal (football 1/2/3/6/7/8, basketball 1/2/3, soccer 1, baseball 1-4), how to word a play, and how much footage to keep around it. Madden's regions were tuned on real footage. Madden 26 and 27 scores are read by matching each digit's shape against templates learned from real games of both (src/madden27.py, scripts/learn_m27_digits.py), which reads the bold HUD font exactly where OCR guessed; plain OCR stays as the fallback and the bot picks the better reader during the first minute of gameplay. A video holding several games back to back becomes one highlight per game (verified on a three-game franchise episode). The other games start from best guesses and are marked not calibrated until a full-frame gameplay screenshot with the score visible is used to set the region (tools/debug_ocr.py shows what the OCR sees).

Setup

  • Python 3.10 venv (venv310), Tesseract at C:\Program Files\Tesseract-OCR, ffmpeg and yt-dlp on PATH.
  • .env: DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN; optional WEB_PORT (8450), PUBLIC_SITE_URL, MAX_UPLOAD_MB.
  • YouTube uploads: client_secret.json + python youtube_auth.py once.
  • The site runs inside the bot on 127.0.0.1:8450 and is published as football.manybumps.com by the shared Cloudflare tunnel (an ingress rule in ~/.cloudflared/config.yml).
  • Tests: venv310\Scripts\python.exe scripts\headless_test.py.
